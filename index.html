Foundry

A CAD bench where every part is a signed distance field: a function that tells any point in space how far it is from the surface. A union is min, a cut is max, and a fillet is a soft minimum. Type a part and it is cast.

        Reference
        

          Sizes are mm. Every solid is centred on the origin, Z is up. The last line is the part.
          
Solids

          

            
box(w, d, h)
block; box(s) is a cube

            
cyl(Ø, h)
cylinder along Z

            
sphere(Ø)

            
cone(Ø1, Ø2, h)
frustum, Ø1 at the bottom

            
torus(Ø, tubeØ)
ring around Z (centreline Ø)

            
hex(flats, h)
hex prism, flats facing ±Y

            
gyroid(cell, wall)
infinite lattice; intersect it with a solid

          

          
Sketches (2D)

          

            
rect(w, h)
rectangle; rect(s) is a square

            
circle(Ø)

            
ngon(n, Ø)
regular polygon, a corner on +X

            
polygon([[x, y], …])
any closed outline

            
arc(Ø, width, °)
curved band with round ends, centred on +X

            
text('ABC', h)
stroke font, A–Z 0–9 - . / + : °

            
.offset(r)
grow (or shrink, r < 0) with round corners

            
.extrude(h, r)
to a solid h tall, edges rounded r

            
.revolve(offset)
spin about Z: x is radius, y is height

            
Sketches take add / cut / and, move(x, y), rot(°), mirror, polar, array, shell, scale.

          

          
Booleans

          

            
a.add(b, blend)
union; blend in mm is optional

            
a.cut(b, blend)
subtract

            
a.and(b, blend)
intersect

            
union(a, b, …)

          

          
Placing

          

            
.move(x, y, z)

            
.rot(x°, y°, z°)
X first, then Y, then Z

            
.mirror('xy')
reflect the + side across the named axes

            
.scale(s)
uniform

          

          
Patterns and forms

          

            
.polar(n)
n copies around Z; the original sits on +X

            
.array(n, dx, dy, dz)
n copies, one step apart

            
.stretch(x, y, z)
lengthen by pulling apart: a cylinder becomes a slot

            
.round(r)
grow by r, rounding every edge

            
.shell(t)
hollow skin t thick

            
.twist(°/mm)
twist about Z

          

          
Inputs

          

            
param(name, v, min, max, step)
a live slider

            
PI sin cos tan sqrt hypot
maths helpers
